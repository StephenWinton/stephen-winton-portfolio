# Leadership Profile

## Enterprise Transformation & Strategic Program Leadership

I am a senior transformation and program leader with 15+ years of experience improving how organizations plan, govern, execute, and measure complex technology work.

My career has combined **program leadership, operating-model design, PMO modernization, delivery transformation, executive reporting, cross-functional alignment, and continuous improvement**. I have led distributed technology teams, supported cloud and infrastructure transformation, improved delivery performance, and built reusable systems that help organizations operate with greater clarity and consistency.

## How I Lead

### Connect Strategy to Execution
I translate broad business priorities into practical operating structures: clear ownership, measurable outcomes, planning cadences, risk visibility, dependency management, and executive reporting.

### Build Scalable Operating Models
I focus on systems that can be repeated across teams rather than one-off fixes. That includes delivery frameworks, reporting standards, planning structures, workflow conventions, and reusable enablement assets.

### Improve Through Data and Visibility
I use delivery metrics, capacity signals, dashboards, and trend analysis to identify friction, improve predictability, and support better decisions at both team and leadership levels.

### Lead Through Influence
Much of my work occurs across functions where direct authority is limited. I partner with engineering leaders, product stakeholders, program managers, and executives to align priorities, surface tradeoffs, and drive adoption.

### Use AI as an Operational Enabler
I have hands-on experience applying AI and automation to planning, reporting, workflow execution, and knowledge work. My emphasis is on practical enterprise adoption: reducing manual effort, improving consistency, and creating repeatable ways of working.
