## My Leadership Approach

I am most effective in environments where the work is important, cross-functional, and difficult to coordinate. I bring structure without unnecessary bureaucracy, use data to improve decisions, and focus on building operating practices that continue to work after the initial transformation effort is complete.

AI is one of the tools I use to improve execution. I view it as an **enabler of transformation—not the transformation itself**.
